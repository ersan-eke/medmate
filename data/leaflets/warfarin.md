## About warfarin
Warfarin is an anticoagulant that makes the blood take longer to clot. It is used to prevent and treat blood clots.

## How and when to take it
Take warfarin once a day, at the same time each day, usually in the evening. Your dose is set by blood tests called INR tests, and it may change often at the start.

If you miss a dose, take it as soon as you remember on the same day. Do not take two doses on the same day.

## Side effects
The main risk with warfarin is bleeding. Tell your doctor if you have nosebleeds that do not stop, bleeding gums, blood in your urine or very heavy periods.

## Other medicines
Many medicines change how warfarin works. Painkillers such as ibuprofen and aspirin can raise the risk of bleeding. Check with a pharmacist before you buy any medicine or herbal remedy.
